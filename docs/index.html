Do You Need an Emergency Root Canal in Sacramento?

An infected tooth rarely gets better on its own. If you notice any of these signs, call us right away.

  • Severe, throbbing, or constant tooth pain
  • Lingering sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Swelling in the gums, cheek, or face
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum
  • A darkened, cracked, or broken tooth
  • Pain when chewing or biting down

If you have facial swelling along with fever or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room or call 911.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about emergency root canals and urgent dental care.

When do I need an emergency root canal?

You may need one if you have severe, throbbing tooth pain, swelling in the gum or face, lingering sensitivity to hot or cold, a darkened tooth, or a pimple-like bump on the gum. These are signs of infection inside the tooth and should be checked right away.

Is an emergency root canal painful?

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so the tooth is numb while we work. Most patients feel relief from the toothache once the infected tissue is removed. Mild soreness afterward is normal and can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication as advised by your dentist.

Can I get a same day appointment?

Yes. We focus on urgent dental appointments and same day care. Call 916-460-9458 and describe your symptoms so we can get you seen as quickly as possible.

What can I do for a toothache until I reach the office?

Rinse gently with warm salt water, apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek, and take over-the-counter pain medication as directed on the label. Avoid very hot, cold, or hard foods. Home care only eases symptoms and does not treat an infection, so call us right away.

Do you treat other dental emergencies besides root canals?

Yes. We handle emergency tooth extractions, dental abscess treatment, broken and chipped teeth, dental trauma, and urgent toothache relief.
